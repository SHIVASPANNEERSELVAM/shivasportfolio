01 / THE INTENT

THE PROBLEM.
THE APPROACH.

Why this project?

Launching a product involves several connected tasks: understanding the market, establishing a brand, deciding how to price, and creating useful listings. Switching between those tasks can fragment the workflow.

The approach

LaunchKart brings those stages into one platform. A multi-agent system coordinates specialized work, with a responsive dashboard for managing AI workflows and monitoring system performance.
