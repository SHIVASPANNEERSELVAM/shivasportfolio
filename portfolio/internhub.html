01 / THE INTENT

THE PROBLEM.
THE APPROACH.

Why this project?

Students, recruiters, and administrators each need different information and actions during recruitment. A single generic interface can make that journey harder to navigate.

The approach

InternHub uses role-specific dashboards and authentication to give each group a dedicated experience, with a connected backend and responsive interfaces across devices.
