01 / THE INTENT

THE PROBLEM.
THE APPROACH.

Why this project?

An engaging browser game needs its movement, collisions, visual feedback, and scoring to work together while remaining accessible without a separate installation.

The approach

Cyber Heist pairs React with Phaser 3 to deliver an interactive cyberpunk game in the browser, with game mechanics, animation, collision detection, and score tracking.

02 / WHAT IT DOES

DETAILS THAT
