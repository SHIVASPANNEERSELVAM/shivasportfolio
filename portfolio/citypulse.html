01 / THE INTENT

THE PROBLEM.
THE APPROACH.

Why this project?

Civic issues are easier to act on when reports include context, location, and a clear record of what happens next. CityPulse brings reporting, AI analysis, and authority review into one connected workflow.

The approach

Citizens submit an issue with a description, an optional image, and location details. Authorities review reports through dashboards, analytics, and a map, while AI adds structured context to support prioritization.

02 / WHAT IT DOES

DETAILS THAT
MAKE IT USEFUL.

01

Citizen reporting

Register, sign in, submit a report, and follow its progress. Reports support descriptions, images, addresses, and geographic coordinates.

02

Structured AI analysis

The AI layer returns a detected category, severity, confidence, explanation, and recommended action. Severity maps to a report priority.

03

Authority workspace

Authorized users can filter reports, change status, add notes, and review the history of a report.

04

Geographic context

A Leaflet map places reports using their coordinates. Marker size reflects severity, and selecting a marker reveals report details.

05

Operational analytics

Dashboard endpoints summarize status, categories, severity, trends, and average resolution time for resolved reports.

06

Configurable AI providers

A common service interface supports Gemini and local Ollama integrations, with a mock provider available for development.
